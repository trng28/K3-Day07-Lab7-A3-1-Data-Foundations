# Residential Life Guideline

## I. Purpose

To help students prepare for residential life at university; develop respect and care for others; make responsible lifestyle choices; share academic, social, and cultural experiences with other residents; learn to live independently in harmony with a diverse community; and engage actively as a resident of VinUniversity campus or other VinUni-provided accommodation.

## II. Scope

Applies to all VinUniversity students residing in VinUni-provided accommodation, including on-campus dormitories and off-campus residences arranged by the University and its partners.

## III. General Policy Principles

### 1. Community Principles

All first-year students are required to reside in the VinUni dormitory as part of building a supportive, collaborative student community. Core values: **Openness** (respect racial, cultural, gender, religious, and sexual-orientation diversity), **Sharing and Respecting** (treat people, property, and environment with care), **Healthy Lifestyle** (eat well, exercise, be kind).

### 2. Residential Life: Support Network

Students can reach out to the Residential Office/Residential Life Officer for guidance, support, or housing-related issues (see Appendices I and II for on-campus/off-campus contacts).

### 3. Noise Policy, Quiet Hours and Out/In Hours

Residents must be conscious of the impact of noise on neighbors; electric guitars/drums are not allowed in residences (use the music practice rooms in the sports complex instead).

| Category | Days | Time | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Quiet Hours | Sunday – Thursday | 10:00 PM – 7:00 AM | Keep noise to a minimum |
| Quiet Hours | Friday – Saturday | 12:00 AM – 7:00 AM | Adjustments may apply during holidays/exam weeks |
| Curfew (on-campus) | All days | After 11:00 PM | Students should remain on campus after this time; late returns may result in no campus access |
| Off-campus routine | All days | No fixed hours | Maintain a safe routine and avoid late-night travel without valid reasons |

Students should stay on campus as much as possible, especially on weekdays. To stay off campus (typically weekends), students must email the Residential Office in advance with departure/return dates. If an off-campus student will be away from their registered accommodation for more than one night, they must inform the Residential Life Officer in advance by email with departure/return dates, reason for absence, and emergency contact information. Proactive communication is mandatory even though approval is not always required; frequent extended absences during the academic week are discouraged.

### 4. Guest Visit Policy

- Residents must register visitors with the Residential Office; guests meet residents in common areas and may not enter the host apartment without co-tenant consent.
- Guests must always be accompanied by the host and must not be given the apartment key; the host is accountable for guest behavior.
- Day guests must leave by 10:00 pm; the residential area does not receive visitors before 7:00 am or after 9:00 pm except emergencies.
- Maximum 3 guests per resident at a time.
- Register via the Guest Visit online form at least 3 working days ahead for day visits and 5 working days ahead for overnight guests.

### 5. Room Change Request

No resident has priority over a particular space; requests are handled case-by-case:

1. Talk to roommate(s) to resolve the conflict, optionally with a Resident Assistant; complete a roommate agreement; allow at least 1 week.
2. If unresolved, meet with the Faculty in Residence (formal meeting, another roommate agreement, another week to resolve).
3. If still unresolved, contact the Residential Office, which manages the dispute and possible room change. Swapping rooms without Residential Office permission is not allowed.

### 6. Move-in and Move-out Procedure

**Check-in:** complete all check-in paperwork (notarized ID/passport+visa copy if not already submitted, campus residence form, property handover form, Temporary Residence Registration Forms) before or at the scheduled move-in time. Taking possession of the room key and signing the handover form indicates acceptance of this Guideline. Residents must check the room within 1 day of receiving the key; unreported issues become the student's responsibility at check-out.

**Check-out:** required after the final exam/assessment week each semester. Residents schedule an inspection time with the Residential Office during move-out week (charges, if any, are split among apartment-mates and paid before departure or deducted from the deposit); register a move-out day/time at least 1 week before departure; clean the space and remove personal items/trash; complete the check-out form and return keys/borrowed items on the move-out day.

### 7. Residents' Code of Conduct

Refer to the Student Code of Conduct Policy and Procedure for details. Loss/damage to a residence room or area is charged based on severity, as judged by the review council. Posting/solicitation in public residential areas requires Student Affairs Management approval; posting on exterior balconies/railings is prohibited; posting inside bedrooms or on interior-facing windows/doors must not obstruct doors/windows or endanger safety; posting in common living areas is permitted unless a co-resident objects. The Residential Office retains the right to enter assigned rooms for maintenance, emergencies, suspected policy/law violations, or safety/sanitation checks; unauthorized items may be confiscated.

### 8. Health and Safety

- **Security:** do not bypass/disable residential security or allow unauthorized building access; lock doors/windows when away or asleep; report suspicious activity to the Residential Office or Security Hotline.
- **Electricity:** maximum 1 electric kettle (2000W) per apartment, removed from the base after use; no unauthorized electricity use; turn off power-consuming devices when leaving; do not use substitute conductive materials for fuses, put wires directly into outlets, place combustible materials near electrical equipment, leave gasoline/combustibles in unauthorized spaces, use electric stoves/incense outside allowed areas, or iron clothes with heated tools in the apartment.
- **Fire safety:** familiarize with posted fire instructions and evacuation plans; on discovering/suspecting a fire, activate the alarm, evacuate via the nearest exit (no elevators, no retrieving belongings), and call 114 once safe. Do not tamper with smoke detectors, alarms, extinguishers, sprinklers, or emergency exits; keep doorways/hallways/stairways clear.
