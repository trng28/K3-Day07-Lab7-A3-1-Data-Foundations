# Student Code of Conduct

## 1. Purpose

### 1.1 Purpose

The Student Code of Conduct outlines the standards of behavior expected from all VinUniversity students as members of a respectful, inclusive, and academically rigorous learning community. It promotes ethical behavior and mutual respect, defines student rights and responsibilities, outlines prohibited conduct and disciplinary consequences, guides responsible decision-making, and supports the University's reputation and public good. The Code is both educational and regulatory, designed to help students grow as responsible global citizens and future leaders.

### 1.2 Scope

Applies to all individuals enrolled at VinUniversity, including undergraduate, graduate, exchange, visiting, dual-degree, and transfer students. It governs behavior on University premises, in University-sponsored off-campus events/internships/fieldwork/study abroad, on affiliated digital platforms (email, LMS, social media), and in off-campus conduct that may adversely affect the University community, its reputation, or the safety of its members. It applies throughout enrollment, including breaks and periods between terms.

## 2. Rights and Responsibilities of Students

### 2.1 Rights of Students

- Enrollment and access to a student ID card and student portal account.
- Respect, transparency, and full information about applicable policies.
- Learning and development support: library/facilities/lab access, research and competition opportunities, healthcare services, exchange/graduate program eligibility, participation in recognized student organizations, support services (career counseling, mental health, special circumstances), and rights to request leave, deferment, acceleration, extension, transfer, or dual degrees.
- Consideration for scholarships and social benefits (e.g., government-mandated public service discounts).
- Feedback and representation: providing feedback, proposing improvements, and filing complaints regarding legitimate rights and interests.
- Consideration for on-campus or off-campus housing.
- Graduation support: receiving degree, transcript, and other academic documents upon fulfilling requirements.

### 2.2 Responsibilities of Students

- Comply with Vietnamese law and VinUni/college regulations; study and practice with integrity and proactive self-study.
- Respect faculty, staff, administrators, and peers; promote solidarity and uphold cultural norms.
- Communicate respectfully and in good faith through official internal channels, refraining from premature escalation (including via social media) while internal resolution is ongoing.
- Preserve university property and reputation.
- Comply with initial and periodic health check-up regulations.
- Fulfill all required payments (tuition, health insurance, mandatory fees) per the Student Financial Regulations and Tariffs, and repay loans/damages as applicable.
- Participate in public service and volunteer activities as required.
- Keep personal information updated on the student portal.
- Comply with military service obligations and National Defense Education requirements (if applicable).
- Fulfill post-graduation scholarship commitments or repay scholarship/fees per agreement.
- Prevent and self-report (within 48 hours of awareness) academic misconduct, dishonesty, or violations of laws/regulations.
- Maintain safety and security on and off campus, and fulfill other obligations under law and University regulations.

### 2.3 Prohibited Acts for Students

- Conduct that insults, demeans, threatens, or infringes on the dignity, honor, or safety of others, including physical assault.
- Academic dishonesty: cheating, unauthorized exam materials, grade manipulation, impersonation, plagiarism/outsourcing of work, proxy testing, research misconduct (falsification, plagiarism, misrepresentation), or any academic fraud.
- Unauthorized use of the "VinUniversity/VinUni" name, logo, or affiliation in publications, media, events, or public statements.
- Creating unofficial VinUni-branded online pages/groups or content that misrepresents or harms the University's reputation.
- Smoking or drinking alcohol on campus; attending class under the influence.
- Organizing/participating in unlawful gatherings, protests, petitions, social evils, illegal street racing, or gambling.
- Producing, trading, transporting, storing, or using weapons, explosives, drugs, banned substances; possessing/disseminating prohibited or pornographic materials; promoting superstition or unauthorized religious activity.
- Establishing/joining illegal political activities or unauthorized collective activities under the University's name.
- Posting/sharing vulgar, violent, pornographic, or unlawful content, or content defaming individuals/organizations online.
- Any other unlawful activity.

## 3. Disciplinable Conduct

### 3.1 Forms of Disciplinary Actions

Depending on nature, severity, frequency, and consequences, students may receive verbal/written reminders, official warnings, or one of the following formal actions:

| Tier 1 | Tier 2 | Tier 3 | Tier 4 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Expression of Disapproval — first-time minor violation | Warning — repeat minor violation, frequent minor violations, or a relatively serious first-time violation | Temporary Suspension — continued violation while under warning, serious violations, or a suspended criminal sentence. Duration: one semester, one academic year, or the length of the suspended sentence | Dismissal/Expulsion — continued violation while suspended, a first-time particularly serious violation causing significant harm, or an unsuspended prison sentence |

Disciplinary actions at warning level or higher are recorded in official records and notified to the student's family; suspension/expulsion also triggers notice to family and local authorities. International students must comply with Vietnamese law and, where applicable, their home country's laws and local rules; violations may result in disciplinary and/or legal consequences.

### 3.2 Procedure for Student Code of Conduct Incidents

- **Tier 1/Tier 2:** Student Affairs Management reviews the case, assesses evidence, and determines the disciplinary action.
- **Tier 3/Tier 4:** the Student Awarding and Disciplinary Committee convenes to assess the case and propose action to the Provost for final approval. Members with a personal interest must recuse themselves. The Committee is chaired by the Vice Provost of Academic Administration, with the Head of Student Affairs Management as Vice Chair (standing member), and representatives from academic departments, administrative offices, the Ho Chi Minh Communist Youth Union, and the Student Council. Cohort representatives, program directors, or academic advisors may be invited to provide input without voting rights.

### 3.3 Mitigating Factors

The Committee may weigh: prior misconduct history, how far the student is into their program, evidence of intent, and personal circumstances (e.g., physical/mental health) affecting decision-making.

### 3.4 Right to Appeal Disciplinary Decisions

A student who believes a decision is unjust may submit a written appeal with evidence to conduct@vinuni.edu.vn within 10 working days of receiving the decision. The Committee will reconsider the case and may invite new members if necessary.

### 3.5 Termination of Disciplinary Decision

- **Expression of Disapproval:** expires automatically after 3 months with no repeat/serious violations, restoring full rights.
- **Warning:** expires automatically after 6 months under the same condition.
- **Temporary Suspension:** upon completion, the student must present local-authority certification of good conduct (and confirmation of any suspended sentence fulfillment) for the University to review reinstatement.
