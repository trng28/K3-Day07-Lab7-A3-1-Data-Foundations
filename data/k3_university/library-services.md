# Library Access & Services Policy

## I. Purpose

To provide guidance for library patrons about the use of library resources, services, and facilities.

## II. General Library Regulations

### 1. General Regulations

#### 1.1 Opening Hours

Library opening hours are subject to change during exam periods, holidays, and summer break and will be posted at the main library entrance and on the library website.

| | Monday-Friday | Saturday-Sunday | Exam/summertime | National holidays |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Main entrance | 8:00 am – 9:00 pm | 9:00 am – 5:00 pm | To be determined | Closed |
| 24/7 learning space | Open 24/7 | Open 24/7 | Open 24/7 | Open 24/7 |
| 2nd floor entrance | 8:30 am – 5:30 pm | Closed (only open on working Saturdays 8:30am-5:30pm) | 8:30 am – 5:30 pm | Closed |

#### 1.2 Library Access

- **VinUni ID required:** only students, faculty, and staff with a valid VinUni ID can enter the library, borrow items, or use electronic resources. IDs must be shown when requested.
- **Non-regular users:** adjunct, affiliated faculty, and eligible Vingroup or partner staff may apply for access via the library website.
- **Access restriction:** library resources and facilities are for VinUni members only. Sharing your VinUni ID or card is strictly forbidden.
- **Security rule:** never open the door for others or leave it propped open. If you notice a breach or have concerns, contact Security at (0247 108 9779 ext. 9901) or alert the on-duty officer.

#### 1.3 Courtesy & Safety Rules

Library users must respect property, maintain cleanliness, be considerate of noise, use equipment safely, and request permission for photo/video shoots (email library@vinuni.edu.vn). Prohibited items/actions: smoking or vaping, animals/bikes/skateboards, food (only spill-proof non-alcoholic drinks allowed), stimulants, weapons, or hazardous substances.

#### 1.4 Lost & Found

Do not leave personal items unattended; you are responsible for any loss or damage. Unattended items, including laptops or borrowed books, may be moved to the Lost & Found at the Student Affairs Management Office.

### 2. Use of Library Materials

#### 2.1 Circulation Regulations

| No. | Material types | Can borrow? | Where to borrow/return | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | General Books | Yes | Self-check machines (1F & 2F), Circulation Desk (1F), 24/7-return-station | |
| 2 | Reference Materials & Journals | No | Not available for borrowing | |
| 3 | CDs/DVDs | Yes | Circulation Desk (1F) | |
| 4 | Course Reserve Books | Yes (2 hours) | Circulation Desk (1F) | 1 item/user/time; short-term use only |

Borrowing rules: return or renew before the due date (overdue items can't be renewed); renewal period equals half of the original loan and is allowed only if no one else has requested the item; report damage before check-out/check-in; a due date falling on a holiday is extended to the next open day; all loans must be returned and fines paid at least 1 month before leaving VinUni. Exceptions to fines or loan terms require approval from VinUni senior leadership.

#### 2.2 Circulation Privileges

| User groups | Number of items | Loan period | Renewals |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Undergraduate Students | 3 | 2 weeks | 1 time |
| Graduate Students | 5 | 1 month | 1 time |
| Faculty (VinUni) | 5 | 6 months | 1 time |
| Affiliated Faculty / Full-time & Service Contract Staff | 3 | 2 weeks | 1 time |
| Visiting Students | Based on library approval | Based on library approval | Based on library approval |
| Guests / Interns / Alumni | Not eligible to borrow | Not eligible to borrow | Not eligible to borrow |

Note: faculty borrowing textbooks for teaching can request a 6-month loan, but items may be recalled if requested by others; extension requests go to library@vinuni.edu.vn.

#### 2.3 Requesting Books or CDs/DVDs

Patrons can request items currently checked out. Requests won't be accepted if the item is in the Course Reserve, is lost/missing/non-circulating, borrowing privileges are blocked, or the borrowing limit has been reached. Once ready, the item is held for 2 days; only the requesting patron can pick it up in person.

#### 2.4 Recalls

All borrowed items can be recalled after 1 week. If recalled, the patron gets an email notice with a new, earlier due date.

#### 2.5 Use of Electronic Resources

Access is limited to VinUni students, faculty, and staff (unless otherwise stated in the license); log in with a VinUni ID to use library databases; materials are for personal, non-commercial use only. Misuse may lead to university-wide access being revoked and disciplinary or legal action.

#### 2.6 Copying, Printing & Scanning

For study, research, and non-commercial use only; users must not violate copyright laws and are responsible for any legal issues caused by improper use.

### 3. Use of Library Property & Spaces

#### 3.1 Borrowing Library Equipment

Loan period is 1 working day; return by 15 minutes before closing time. Items overdue more than 5 days are considered lost and users must pay for replacements. Equipment must be returned in person at the circulation desk (1F). Fine waivers or loan extensions require approval from university leadership.

#### 3.2 Using Library Functional Rooms (group rooms, studios, AR/VR room)

Book in advance (same day or up to 1 week ahead via Microsoft Outlook, first come first served); academic use only; maximum 2 hours/session, 2 sessions/day (all rooms combined); bookings are cancelled after 10 minutes of no-show.

### 4. Library Regulation Violations

#### 4.1 Consequences

Students who break library rules may face penalties based on VinUni's Student Code of Conduct.

#### 4.2 Library Fines

Fines apply for returning items late or for damaged/lost materials/equipment, following VinUni's Financial Regulations and Tariff.

| Types | Examples |
| --- | --- |
| Minor damage | Small tears or stains on <10 pages, light cover damage, light water marks, minor equipment issues (checked by IT) |
| Major damage/loss | Missing or heavily damaged cover/pages, lots of writing or stains, bad odor, broken or unreadable CDs/DVDs, broken or lost equipment |

No refunds if a lost item is found after the replacement fee has been paid. Email reminders are sent, but not receiving one does not excuse late returns. Fines may be waived only in serious cases (e.g., illness or hospitalization, proof needed). To appeal a fine, email library@vinuni.edu.vn (decided case by case).
