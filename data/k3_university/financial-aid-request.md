# Guidelines for Student Financial Aid Support Request

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This document provides a clear process for students to request financial support, ensuring fairness and transparency in the allocation of funds. Financial support is available for tuition fees, living expenses, and emergency needs, depending on eligibility and availability of resources.

Financial Support Programs under the Financial Aid Office's management:

| Nr. | Key Financial Aid Programs | Coverage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | VinUni Need-Based Financial Aid – Regular Financial Assistance | Tuition fees |
| 2 | Global Exchange Student Ambassador Grant – Exchange programs | Non-tuition fees for exchange programs (travel, visa application, housing, insurance, etc.) |
| 3 | Student Support Assistance – Emergency Financial Assistance | On-campus housing subsidy, travel expenses, meals, etc., or tuition fee (for special cases) |

The Merit-based scholarship, Student Research Grant, Start-up Grant, and Work-Study Program fall outside of the Financial Aid Office's guidelines; the respective departments manage these programs.

## 2. Eligibility Criteria

Students must meet all of the following criteria to be eligible for financial support:

- Enrollment in a full-time academic program at VinUni.
- Demonstration of financial need through supporting documentation.
- Satisfactory academic progress (not at-risk academic standing).
- No major disciplinary action (Level 3 and up, per the Student Code of Conduct) at the time of application submission.
- Completion of the necessary application forms.
- Any other special circumstances (e.g., family emergencies, loss of income) must be clearly outlined.

## 3. Timelines

| Application period | Processing deadline | Effective semester |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 20 June – 10 July | 02 August | Fall semester |

Note: special cases may be considered. Applications for the Global Exchange Student Ambassador (GESA) Grant depend on the nomination timelines of the VinUni Global Exchange Office.

## 4. Financial Support Request Procedures

| Steps | Action | Detailed Explanation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Gather required documentation | Completed Financial Support Application Form, proof of financial need, additional supporting documents (e.g., letters explaining special circumstances or medical bills). |
| 2 | Submit financial support request | Submit online through the student portal with all documentation attached. Deadlines: July 10th (Fall) and November 10th (Spring) each year. GESA Grant: after nomination for an exchange program. |
| 3 | Initial review by Financial Aid Office (FAO) | FAO reviews for completeness and eligibility; incomplete applications are flagged within 3 working days. |
| 4 | Review by Financial Aid Review Committee | Committee of university staff and faculty evaluates financial need, academic standing, resource availability, special circumstances, and grant-specific eligibility. Review duration: 5 working days after receiving the request from FAO. |
| 5 | Notification of decision | Students are notified via email or the student portal within 2 working days of the Committee's decision (approved/denied/further documentation needed, amount awarded, next steps). |
| 6 | Disbursement of funds | Tuition fees: applied directly to the billing statement. Non-tuition fees: disbursed to the student's VND bank account (or an authorized parent/relative's account with official authorization). Follows current university financial and payment regulations. |

## 5. Appeals Process

If a student's request is denied or they wish to appeal the awarded amount, they may submit an Appeal Form within 5 working days of receiving the decision, including a written statement explaining the reason for the appeal and any new supporting documentation. The Financial Aid Review Committee reviews the appeal and provides a final decision within 10 working days.

## 6. Renewal of Financial Support

Financial support is awarded for one regular semester, one academic year, or as a one-time aid unless otherwise specified. Renewal requires a new application submitted by the deadline with updated financial documentation, following the same steps as the original request. The GESA grant is a one-time financial support grant; students can only receive it once during their time at VinUni.

## 7. Disbursement of Funds

Grants are disbursed prior to the issuance of the tuition fee schedule (for tuition fee aid) and within 2 working weeks or the nearest payment cycle (for cash grants), according to VinUni's financial regulations.
