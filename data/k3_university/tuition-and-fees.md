# Financial Regulations and Tariff (for student)

## A. Financial Regulations and Tariff for Undergraduate

### I. Listed Tuition Fee

Listed tuition fee is issued according to the standard curriculum framework announced at the time of admission. Tuition fees include training-practical fees, facilities fees, IT support, and student activities (orientation, health services and counseling psychology, academic advisors, career counseling, student associations, clubs, sporting-cultural events, international exchange, business experiences).

| Program | Standard duration (years) | Listed tuition fee per academic year | Per semester | Per credit (*) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Bachelor of Nursing | 4 | 349,650,000 VND/year | 174,825,000 VND/semester | 9,780,000 VND/credit |
| Doctor of Medicine | 6 | 815,850,000 VND/year | 407,925,000 VND/semester | 27,195,000 VND/credit |
| Other Bachelor Programs | 4 | 815,850,000 VND/year | 407,925,000 VND/semester | 27,195,000 VND/credit |

(*) Per-credit tuition applies to cases such as: double-degree/double-minor additional credits, integrated exchange or visiting-student credits, short-term exchange programs, course retakes, and credit-transfer students who need additional courses (from AY2024–2025 onwards).

Tuition fees do not distinguish between training methods (online/offline/hybrid) and apply the same rate to Vietnamese and international students. Payment deadline: tuition is paid twice a year at the beginning of each main semester, per the annual schedule announced by VinUni.

### II. Other Fees

- **Application fee:** 2,000,000 VND/application/applicant for applicants accepted to interview; waived for recruitment-event/competition participants, high-school principal nominees, or other approved special cases. Not refundable or transferable.
- **Confirmation (Retention) Fee for Scholarship/Tuition Discount/Financial Aid Eligibility:** 20,000,000 VND/time/student, payable per the University's announcement. Not refundable or transferable, but deductible from tuition upon enrollment; refundable only if the offer was conditional and not met, or the University cancels the intake. Reconciled after the first academic year for students with 100%+ tuition scholarships.
- **Library fee — overdue fines:** 10,000 VND/day overdue/document (normal material), 10,000 VND/hour overdue/document (course reserve), 10,000 VND/day overdue/equipment. Students with unpaid fines cannot continue borrowing. Items overdue more than 30 days incur an additional fine equal to the cover price.
- **Library fee — repair of slightly damaged items:** 200,000 VND/document or equipment, plus any applicable overdue fines.
- **Library fee — replacement of lost/badly damaged items:** market-available items: purchase price + 200,000 VND admin fee; items unavailable on the market: 5× the original price; equipment: replacement price + 200,000 VND admin fee. Fees are collected within 30 working days of notification.
- **Academic Administrative Fee:**

| Certified documents (transcript, confirmation, copies) | 50,000 VND/copy | At time of request |

| Processing Program Transfer | 2,000,000 VND/request | Upon receipt of application |

| Exam Score Review | 1,000,000 VND/request/subject | Upon receipt of application |

- **Re-examination and retake fees:** retaken-course tuition is 50% of the standard per-credit rate. Re-examination fees: theoretical exams 1,500,000 VND/attempt; practical exams 2,500,000 VND/attempt; NBME-standard exams 2,000,000 VND/attempt. These fees are not eligible for tuition discounts, early-payment incentives, or scholarships. For cohorts 1–5 (admitted before AY2025), the first retake/re-exam of a course first registered before Fall 2025 remains free; the fee applies from the second retake/re-exam of that course, or for any course first registered from Fall 2025 onward. Cohort 6 and later: the new fee policy applies to all attempts.
- **Dormitory fee:** covers accommodation, facilities, internet, common restroom, technical fee, parking (1 vehicle/student, advance registration required), electricity (120 kWh/person/month) and water (4 m³/person/month), with excess usage charged at the prescribed rate. Calculated by the number of residence days per the official move-in/move-out dates.

  The Preferential Rate applies only if the student resided in the dormitory at least 9 months in the immediately preceding academic year and registers for a new stay of at least one semester; it can be combined with the payment discount policy. Each resident pays a deposit equal to one month's rent, billed with the dormitory fee. Deadlines: short-term students pay in full within 1 week before admission to the dormitory; full-time students pay together with tuition. Refunds: dormitory fee is refundable only for periods the student could not stay due to objective reasons from VinUniversity (not for disciplinary reasons); voluntary withdrawal follows the same refund rate as tuition (see Section D.4). Deposit is refundable when leaving for objective reasons, after deducting outstanding receivables.
- **Compulsory Health Insurance Fee:** collected and paid on behalf of students — compulsory for Vietnamese students (maintained even during exchange or approved leave of absence) unless they already hold valid coverage (submit proof to clinic@vinuni.edu.vn); for international students it is a pass-through fee for the University-selected provider (PVI Insurance), paid once per year with first-semester tuition, with no markup.
- **General Health Examination Fee:** mandatory annual check-up per Ministry of Health regulation, pass-through fee paid once per year with student billing; students who skip the University's organized check-up bear the cost of an external examination and must submit results to the University Clinic.
- **Graduation Package Fee:** 2,000,000 VND/student, covering regalia, individual photo shoot, and event logistics; all-inclusive and non-refundable even if not all services are used.
- **Compensation Fee for Property Loss or Damage:** charged immediately upon occurrence, based on a fee schedule for specific items (e.g., graduation gown 1,500,000 VND, mattress 6,000,000 VND, microwave 3,700,000 VND) or, for other property, the purchase/repair cost plus a 200,000 VND administrative fee per incident.

## B. Financial Regulations and Tariff for Postgraduate

Postgraduate programs (Resident Doctor, Master's, PhD) have a separate application fee (2,000,000 VND, waived for specific cases), a listed tuition fee of 932,400,000 VND/year (varying by program duration), and program-specific scholarships/financial aid. The IFOM exam fee for the Resident Doctor Program is 2,000,000 VND/person, subject to annual adjustment. Other fees follow Section A.II; students with 100% tuition sponsorship are exempted from the Confirmation/Retention Fee.

## C. Financial Regulations and Tariff for Other Student Services

Exchange students from partner universities pay a facilities-use fee: 1,250,000 VND/week/student (short-term, under 1 semester/4 months) or 10,000,000 VND/semester/student (long-term, 1 semester or more), collected once per semester together with dormitory/other service fees. Not refundable or transferable; other rules follow the partner agreement.

## D. Tuition Fees, Scholarship and Financial Aid Policies & Other Assistances

### 1. Merit Scholarship and Financial Aid Policy

Students enrolled until 2030 receive an Educational Development Grant equivalent to a 35% discount on listed tuition for the program's official duration, already included within the Merit-based Scholarship (50%–full tuition) and Financial Aid packages. Combined support (Talent Scholarship + Additional Scholarship + Financial Support, including the 35% grant) cannot exceed 100% of the listed tuition fee. The Talent Scholarship applies for the entire duration of study and is not eligible for upgrade. Scholarship/aid maintenance follows the Guidelines for Maintaining Entry Scholarship and Financial Aid Support.

### 2. Special Tuition Fee Assistances and Payment Discounts

- **Family Tuition Assistance:** 5% reduction on listed tuition when a sibling, parent, spouse, or legal child is also enrolled at VinUni (from the second family member onward); requires a supporting application submitted at least 1 week before the fee notification date. Adjusted if the family member discontinues studies (with reimbursement owed by the student).
- **Alumni Tuition Assistance:** 10% reduction on listed tuition for VinUni alumni pursuing a second undergraduate degree or continuing a graduate program; requires an application at least 1 week before the fee notification date.
- **Annual Payment Discount:** 5% discount on the total tuition and dormitory fees paid upfront for the full academic year, if paid within the announced deadline.
- **Conditions:** Alumni and Family Tuition Assistance cannot be combined with each other, but either can be combined with scholarship programs and the Annual Payment Discount. Effective from Fall Semester 2025.

### 3. Tuition Fees and Other Adjustments

A student's tuition fee does not change during the course, including the retention period. Other fees may change per University policy and Vietnamese law.

### 4. Tuition Retention and Refund Policy

Tuition and other payments are non-deferrable and non-refundable except as permitted under this Regulation and approved by the University. Leave-of-absence/withdrawal/drop-out applications must be submitted at least 30 days before the official withdrawal date.

- **Tuition Retention:** applies if the leave-of-absence application is submitted by the last working day of week 3 of the semester and approved; the paid tuition is reserved for up to 1 academic year and deducted from future payable fees on return. Late deferment requests are not retained except in special cases (military/mobilization duty, serious illness, accident/force majeure, with full documentation).
- **Tuition Refund:** full refund for natural disaster, war, terrorism, or other serious crises forcing full closure without alternative learning. Partial refund for voluntary, approved withdrawal: 80% refund if withdrawing before the official start date of the semester/short course; 50% refund if withdrawing within 2 weeks of the start date; no refund after 2 weeks from the start date. Each semester is assessed separately for timing and refund rate.
- Changes to learning format/schedule/mode (e.g., offline to online) are considered a normal continuation of study and are not grounds for tuition refund, deferral, or reduction.

## E. Other Related Policies

### Deadlines, Payment Methods, and Terms of Implementation

- Payment deadlines are specified per fee type above; extensions require approved evidence of objective reasons, and the extended deadline must be before the first day of the semester.
- **Payment methods:** online payment platform (https://my.vinuni.edu.vn/); USD account for international transfers (contact financialsupport@vinuni.edu.vn); bank card at the Finance–Accounting Office; or wire transfer to VinUniversity's Techcombank account (VND: 88991188; USD: 11229922). USD tuition payment is only for international students, converted at Techcombank's buying rate on the Payment Notice date.
- **Late payment:** a 2,000,000 VND late payment fee applies, paid together with the tuition fee. Payment is recorded on the date funds are received per VinUni's Bank Credit Note.
- If unpaid for 1–3 weeks past the deadline (or approved extension), students are suspended from course registration, withdrawn from enrolled courses, and lose access to Canvas, Library, SIS, and other systems. Students with outstanding non-tuition fees may be restricted from transcripts, status confirmations, dormitory priority, and other services; international students risk immigration status reporting.
- If a student is suspended for non-payment for two consecutive semesters and still fails to pay in the following semester, they are forced to withdraw; prior debts must be paid in full before reapplying. Students returning from leave with outstanding obligations can only re-enroll once all obligations are settled.
- Violations of financial policy may result in discipline from reprimand up to dismissal; the University may pursue external debt recovery under Vietnamese law. All financial obligations must be settled before graduation is considered.
- **Settlement of receivables/payables:** refunds (e.g., dormitory deposit, overpayment) are netted against outstanding receivables at year-end and prioritized as credit for the next academic year.
- **Electronic invoices:** the official e-invoice is sent to the student's VinUniversity email within 30 days of payment; special invoicing to an organization requires a request submitted before the payment deadline; fees collected on behalf of third parties (e.g., compulsory health insurance) are not included in the e-invoice.
